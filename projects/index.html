Projects

Current research programmes and developing open-science resources.

Research projects

Compound heat and PM₂.₅ risk

Spatial assessment of coincident extreme heat and particulate pollution, their drivers, and the populations facing elevated joint risk. Read the publication.

Aerosols and heat extremes

Analysis of how absorbing aerosols influence radiation, regional temperature, heatwave behaviour, and dry and moist heat stress over India.

PM₂.₅ health and environmental justice

Sector-specific modelling of particulate exposure and attributable mortality across rural and urban India, including socioeconomic vulnerability.

India-InMAP development

Collaborative development of a reduced-complexity air-quality model for rapid evaluation of emission-control strategies and health benefits across India.

Black-carbon statistical metrics

An IIT Bombay–Duke University collaboration connecting absorbing-aerosol indicators with extreme temperature, health, labour productivity, and changing policy frameworks.

ACTIONN

An ACCESS Award-winning mitigation framework linking clean cooking, carbon finance, rural entrepreneurship, and climate-aligned livelihoods.

Open-science roadmap

  • WRF-Chem evaluation and diagnostic workflows for India
  • Aerosol-radiative-forcing and temperature-response utilities
  • PM₂.₅ health and rural–urban equity analysis
  • Earth-system and geospatial visualisation examples

Code and documentation will be released through GitHub as the projects mature.
