Research mission

From atmospheric processes to human consequences.

I combine coupled atmospheric modelling, climate-model experiments, observations, health-impact assessment, and geospatial analytics to translate complex Earth-system signals into evidence for cleaner and more equitable policy.

01 · Climate

Aerosols and heat extremes

Understanding how absorbing aerosols alter radiation, meteorology, and dry and moist heat stress across India.

02 · Air quality

Sources, exposure, and mortality

Tracing sectoral PM₂.₅ contributions from emissions through exposure to attributable health burdens.

03 · Equity

Rural–urban vulnerability

Locating unequal risks and connecting air-quality interventions with socioeconomic vulnerability.

Research system

One connected analytical pipeline.

My work follows the full chain from emissions and atmospheric response to population exposure, health impacts, and distributional equity.
