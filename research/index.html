Research

I study interactions among air pollution, climate extremes, population exposure, health, and socioeconomic vulnerability.

Research interests

  • Aerosol–radiation–meteorology interactions
  • Absorbing aerosols and dry and moist heat extremes
  • WRF-Chem air-quality modelling and source attribution
  • PM₂.₅ exposure and attributable mortality
  • Rural–urban disparities and environmental justice
  • Compound heat–pollution risk
  • Reduced-complexity modelling and policy assessment
