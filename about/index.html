Dewashish Tiwari

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, working with Prof. Chandra Venkataraman at the intersection of atmospheric science, climate change, air quality, and environmental health.

My research investigates how aerosols affect regional climate and extreme heat, how emissions from different sectors influence PM₂.₅ exposure and attributable mortality, and how these impacts vary across rural and urban communities.

I combine WRF-Chem and climate-model simulations with satellite observations, reanalysis products, health-impact assessment, and geospatial analysis. I am particularly interested in compound heat–pollution risks, absorbing aerosols, environmental justice, and reproducible Earth-system data science.

I am completing my doctoral work and am open to postdoctoral and research collaborations in atmospheric modelling, climate extremes, air quality, and environmental health. You can find more detail on my research page and public CV.
